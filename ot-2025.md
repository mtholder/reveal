### Current Status

  * Open Tree Taxonomy version 3.7
    * 4.5 million taxa (including dubious taxa)
    * 3.7 million taxa
    * 2.2 million synonyms
  * Over 5190 studies curated by over 300 curators
  * Synthetic tree version 15.1
    * 1,362 input phylogenies included
    * 2.38 million tips



### Active work

  * Working on "custom" (*vs* full) synthesis of select taxa with that have had more curation (e.g. <a href="https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.05.20.595017v1" target="_blank">Birds</a>)
  * Working to migrate away from Open Tree Taxonomy to the upcoming <a href="https://discourse.gbif.org/t/switching-gbif-s-taxonomic-backbone-to-the-catalogue-of-life-extended-release-x-release-gbif-technical-support-hour-for-nodes/5417" target="_blank">Catalogue of Life XRelease</a>
  * This will allow tight interaction with <a href="https://www.gbif.org/" target="_blank">GBIF</a> for phylogenetic views of biodiversity data.
